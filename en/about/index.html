Psychology Meets Digital Innovation

Psychologist | Certified Positive Psychotherapist | Digital Mental Health

I am Alona Perederiy, a psychologist and Certified Positive Psychotherapist working at the intersection of psychological practice, mental health innovation and digital technology.

My professional interests extend beyond individual therapy. I am particularly interested in how digital platforms can make psychological support more accessible, structured and relevant to people's everyday lives.

As a Co-founder of SympathiQ, I bring a psychological perspective to digital mental health initiatives, combining an understanding of human behaviour with an interest in product development, user experience and sustainable wellbeing solutions.

Alongside my work in digital mental health, I maintain an independent psychological practice, supporting individuals through emotional challenges, relationships and significant life transitions.
