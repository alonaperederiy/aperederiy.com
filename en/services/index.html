My Professional Approach

My practice is informed by Positive Psychotherapy, an integrative approach that considers individual strengths, personal resources, relationships and the wider context of a person's life.

I aim to provide a thoughtful, collaborative and individually tailored process, combining professional psychological knowledge with respect for each person's unique experiences and circumstances.
